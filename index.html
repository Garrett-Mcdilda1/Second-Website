Project #1: Senior Design

This is my senior design project where we built a steam blancher for teaching food science students unsteady state heat transfer. My primary technical task was to develop an electric safety shutoff system that prevents students from burning themselves. The system works such that all doors are required to be closed before a solenoid will allow steam to flow into the machine. This will prevent students from being exposed to presurized steam.
